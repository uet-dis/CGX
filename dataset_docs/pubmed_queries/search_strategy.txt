# PubMed Central Search Strategy: Heart Failure (Cumulative Filter Pipeline)

This document describes the 5-stage cumulative filtering process used to extract 50 source documents from PMC for the CGX system.

## Stage 1: Base Query (32,415 results)
**Objective:** Collect broad heart failure knowledge (2020-2025).
**Query:**
```sql
("Heart Failure"[MeSH Terms] OR "Heart Failure"[Title/Abstract] OR "Cardiac Failure"[Title/Abstract] OR "Heart Decompensation"[Title/Abstract]) AND ("Guideline"[PT] OR "Practice Guideline"[PT] OR "Systematic Review"[PT] OR "Meta-Analysis"[PT]) AND ("2020/01/01"[PDAT] : "2025/12/31"[PDAT]) AND "english"[LA]
```

## Stage 2: Narrowing by Publication Type (17,450 results)
**Objective:** Focus exclusively on official clinical practice guidelines.
**Query:**
```sql
(STAGE_1_RESULTS) AND ("Guideline"[Publication Type] OR "Practice Guideline"[Publication Type])
```

## Stage 3: Filtering by Elite Journals (2,815 results)
**Objective:** Filter by a list of high-impact journals available on the PMC system.
**Query:**
```sql
(STAGE_2_RESULTS) AND ("Eur J Heart Fail"[Journal] OR "ESC Heart Fail"[Journal] OR "J Am Coll Cardiol"[Journal] OR "Circulation"[Journal] OR "JAMA Netw Open"[Journal] OR "Front Cardiovasc Med"[Journal] OR "Sci Rep"[Journal])
```

## Stage 4: Strict Title Filtering (214 results)
**Objective:** Ensure heart failure is the primary focus and the content covers management/treatment.
**Query:**
```sql
("Heart Failure"[Title]) AND ("Management"[Title] OR "Guidelines"[Title] OR "Guideline"[Title] OR "Consensus"[Title] OR "Treatment"[Title] OR "Therapy"[Title]) AND ("Practice Guideline"[PT] OR "Guideline"[PT]) AND ("2020/01/01"[PDAT] : "2025/12/31"[PDAT]) AND ("Eur Heart J"[Journal] OR "J Am Coll Cardiol"[Journal] OR "Circulation"[Journal] OR "Eur J Heart Fail"[Journal] OR "ESC Heart Fail"[Journal] OR "JACC Heart Fail"[Journal] OR "Circ Heart Fail"[Journal]) AND "english"[LA]
```

## Stage 5: Manual Screening (Final 50 Papers)
Direct selection from the 214 results in Stage 4 to identify the final 50 articles, sorted by publication date (PMCID list stored in `final_pmc_list.txt`).

---
*Note: All commands are designed to be run directly on the PubMed Central (PMC) search engine.*
